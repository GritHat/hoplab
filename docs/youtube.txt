TITLE

hoplab — a network laboratory in your terminal (every packet, every layer, 78 labs)

DESCRIPTION

hoplab emulates a whole network inside one terminal program: hosts, routers and switches exchange real, byte-exact frames over links with delay, loss and queues. Watch packets cross the map, open any of them down to the bytes, read every node's tables, plot what the protocols measure, break things and see what happens.

Source code, labs and study guide:
https://github.com/GritHat/hoplab

What is in it
• 30+ protocols and tools: ARP, switching, VLANs, spanning tree and RSTP, IPv4 and IPv6 (Neighbor Discovery, SLAAC, path MTU), RIP and RIPng, OSPF and OSPFv3, BGP with multiprotocol extensions, NAT, queue management (RED, CoDel, fair queueing), UDP, TCP with SACK and congestion control (Reno, CUBIC, BBR), load balancing, DNS, DHCP and DHCPv6, HTTP, error-correcting codes, medium access, Raft, gossip and SWIM, vector clocks, two-phase commit, Chord, CRDTs
• 78 ready-made labs in four levels, from the first ping to consensus algorithms
• a study guide by level and topic, readable at three depths, in English and Italian
• one window, tiled windows as separate processes, or several machines
• C11, no dependencies: make && ./hoplab

Chapters
0:00 Real frames on an emulated network: ARP, ping, sequence diagram, packet dissection
0:18 TCP congestion control: the sawtooth, live
0:28 Routing and consensus: OSPF floods link state, Raft elects a leader
0:46 The catalog of labs and the study guide
1:00 One process per node, joined by UDP tunnels

Music: "Round Trip", written for this video and synthesised by a script in the repository (tools/media/music.py).
The whole video is recorded from the real program by tools/media/record.py.

TITOLO

hoplab — un laboratorio di reti nel terminale (ogni pacchetto, ogni livello, 78 laboratori)

DESCRIZIONE

hoplab emula un'intera rete dentro un solo programma da terminale: host, router e switch si scambiano frame reali, esatti al byte, su collegamenti con ritardo, perdita e code. Si guardano i pacchetti attraversare la mappa, si apre ciascuno fino ai byte, si leggono le tabelle di ogni nodo, si tracciano i grafici di ciò che i protocolli misurano, si rompe qualcosa e si osserva che cosa succede.

Codice sorgente, laboratori e guida di studio:
https://github.com/GritHat/hoplab

Che cosa contiene
• oltre 30 protocolli e strumenti: ARP, switching, VLAN, spanning tree e RSTP, IPv4 e IPv6 (Neighbor Discovery, SLAAC, path MTU), RIP e RIPng, OSPF e OSPFv3, BGP con estensioni multiprotocollo, NAT, gestione delle code (RED, CoDel, fair queueing), UDP, TCP con SACK e controllo di congestione (Reno, CUBIC, BBR), bilanciamento del carico, DNS, DHCP e DHCPv6, HTTP, codici a correzione d'errore, accesso al mezzo, Raft, gossip e SWIM, orologi vettoriali, commit a due fasi, Chord, CRDT
• 78 laboratori pronti su quattro livelli, dal primo ping agli algoritmi di consenso
• una guida di studio per livello e argomento, leggibile a tre profondità, in italiano e in inglese
• una finestra, finestre affiancate come processi separati, oppure più macchine
• C11, senza dipendenze: make && ./hoplab

Il video è in inglese; l'interfaccia del programma è disponibile anche in italiano (HOPLAB_LANG=it).
